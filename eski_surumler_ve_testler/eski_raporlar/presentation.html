🏗️ Model Mimarileri

🧠 LSTM: 2x LSTM layers (128→64) + Dropout (0.3) + Dense → ~138K parameters

🔷 CNN: 3x Conv1D (64→128→64) + MaxPooling + BatchNorm → ~100K parameters

💚 LightGBM: Histogram-based GBDT, 1000 trees, leaf-wise growth

🔴 XGBoost: Regularized boosting, 500 trees, hist method
